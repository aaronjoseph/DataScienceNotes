Financial ratios turn raw figures from the [[Balance Sheet]], [[P&L Statement]] and [[Cash Flow Statement]] into comparable measures. They can be loosely grouped into four categories:

1. [[Profitability Ratios]] — how well the company turns sales and capital into profit.
2. [[Leverage Ratios]] — how much the company relies on debt and whether it can service it.
3. [[Valuation Ratios]] — how the share price compares with earnings, sales or book value.
4. [[Operating Ratios]] — how efficiently the company uses its assets.

Desirable levels vary by industry, so compare a company with its peers and its own history rather than with a universal threshold.[^sec-guide] [[Current Ratio|Liquidity ratios]] such as the current ratio are sometimes treated as a fifth group.
