Stock screening filters the market down to the stocks that meet predefined criteria, leaving a smaller pool to research in depth. It is the first step of the [[Stock Selection Methodology]].

## Author's Screening Criteria

1. **Size:** large and mid caps only, with market capitalisation above ₹6,000 crore.
2. **Growth:** 3-year CAGR of sales and profit above 7%.
3. **Use of capital:**
   - [[Return on Capital Employed (RoCE)]] above 15%.
   - [[Debt to Equity Ratio]] below 0.5.
4. **Valuation:**
   - Current [[Price to Earnings Ratio|P/E]] below 1.5 times the 5-year average P/E.
   - [[PEG Ratio]] below 2; lower is better.

## Worked Example

**Inputs:** a company with a current P/E of 27 and a 5-year average P/E of 20.

**Step 1: valuation threshold.**

$$
1.5 \times 20 = 30
$$

The current P/E of 27 is below 30, so the stock passes this test. The screen only narrows the list; each survivor still needs the [[Stock Selection Checklist]] and a valuation.
