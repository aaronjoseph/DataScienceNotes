Technical indicators are heuristic or pattern-based signals calculated from the price, volume or open interest of a security or contract. Traders who follow technical analysis use them to try to anticipate future price moves. They describe past trading, not the company's fundamentals, and their predictive value is contested.

## Example: Simple Moving Average

The $n$-day simple moving average (SMA) is the mean of the last $n$ closing prices $P$:

$$
\text{SMA}_t = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=0}^{n-1} P_{t-i}
$$

## Worked Example

**Inputs:** five closing prices of 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14.

**Step 1: 5-day SMA.**

$$
\frac{10 + 11 + 12 + 13 + 14}{5} = 12
$$

The latest close of 14 is above its 5-day average of 12, which trend followers read as upward momentum. A moving average lags the price by construction, so it confirms trends rather than predicting turns.
