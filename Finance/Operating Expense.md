Operating expenses are the costs of running the business day to day that cannot be tied directly to producing the goods or services sold, such as administrative salaries, research and marketing.[^sec-guide] They are deducted from [[Gross Profit]] to reach [[Operating Income]].

## Main Components

- [[Selling, General and Administrative Expense]] (SG&A).
- [[R&D Costs]].
- Depreciation and amortisation of non-production assets; see [[Amortization & Depreciation]].

[[Cost of Goods Sold]] is not an operating expense in this sense; it is deducted earlier, to reach gross profit.

## Worked Example

**Inputs:** SG&A 2,100, R&D 1,000 and D&A 1,690; gross profit 7,000.

**Step 1: total operating expenses.**

$$
2{,}100 + 1{,}000 + 1{,}690 = 4{,}790
$$

**Step 2: operating income.**

$$
7{,}000 - 4{,}790 = 2{,}210
$$

Operating expenses consume about two-thirds of gross profit here, which is why a high gross margin alone does not guarantee strong earnings.
