Operating ratios, also called activity or management ratios, measure how efficiently a business turns its assets, current and non-current, into revenue. They reflect how well management runs the company.

## Worked Example

**Inputs:** revenue 10,000, average net fixed assets 5,000 and average total assets 12,000.

**Step 1: fixed assets turnover.**

$$
\frac{10{,}000}{5{,}000} = 2
$$

**Step 2: total assets turnover.**

$$
\frac{10{,}000}{12{,}000} \approx 0.83
$$

Each unit of fixed assets supports two units of sales, but across all assets the company generates only 0.83 of sales per unit, so a large share of its assets is outside the fixed-asset base (working capital, cash or investments). Compare with peers and the company's history.
