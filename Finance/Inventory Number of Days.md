Inventory number of days (days inventory outstanding) is the average number of days a company holds inventory before selling it. Fewer days is generally better: cash is tied up for less time and stock is less likely to go obsolete. It is one of the [[Operating Ratios]].

## Formula

$$
\text{Inventory days} = \frac{365}{\text{Inventory turnover}}
$$

## Worked Example

**Inputs:** an [[Inventory Turnover]] of 3.78.

**Step 1: days.**

$$
\frac{365}{3.78} \approx 96.6
$$

Goods sit in the warehouse for about three months on average. Compare with peers, because a grocer and a jet-engine maker naturally differ by orders of magnitude.
