The debt-to-asset ratio shows what share of a company's assets is financed by debt. It is one of the [[Leverage Ratios]].

## Formula

$$
\text{Debt to assets} = \frac{\text{Total debt}}{\text{Total assets}}
$$

Total debt here means interest-bearing borrowings, short- and long-term. Some sources use total liabilities instead, which gives a higher ratio; state which one you use.

## Worked Example

**Inputs:** total debt 300 and total assets 1,200.

**Step 1: ratio.**

$$
\frac{300}{1{,}200} = 0.25
$$

A quarter of the assets are funded by borrowing. The higher the ratio, the more leveraged and riskier the company, because interest and principal must be paid regardless of how the business performs.
