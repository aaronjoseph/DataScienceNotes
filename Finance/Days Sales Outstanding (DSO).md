Days sales outstanding (DSO) is the average number of days a company takes to collect payment after a sale. It converts the [[Accounts Receivable Turnover Ratio]] into days.

## Formula

$$
\text{DSO} = \frac{365}{\text{Receivables turnover}}
$$

## Worked Example

**Inputs:** a receivables turnover of 10.

**Step 1: DSO.**

$$
\frac{365}{10} = 36.5 \text{ days}
$$

On average, customers pay about five weeks after the sale. A DSO well above the company's stated credit terms, or rising year after year, suggests collection problems or aggressive revenue recognition.
