Profit before tax (PBT), also called earnings before tax (EBT), pre-tax profit or income before tax, is profit after all operating expenses **and interest**, but before income tax.[^sec-guide]

The earlier version said PBT is before deducting interest; that describes EBIT (roughly [[Operating Income]]).

## Formula

$$
\text{PBT} = \text{Operating income} - \text{Interest expense} \pm \text{Other items}
$$

Equivalently, PBT is total revenue minus all expenses other than tax.

## Worked Example

**Inputs:** operating income 2,210, interest expense 700 and a loss on asset sales of 10 (the [[P&L Statement]] example).

**Step 1: PBT.**

$$
2{,}210 - 700 - 10 = 1{,}500
$$

The tax charge is then applied to this 1,500 to reach [[Profit After Tax (PAT)]]; comparing the two is the check in [[Income Tax Paid]].
