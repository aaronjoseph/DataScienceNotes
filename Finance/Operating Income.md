Operating income (income from operations, often close to EBIT) is the profit from the core business after production costs and operating expenses, but before interest and income tax.[^sec-guide]

## Formulas

**Operating income.**

$$
\text{Operating income} = \text{Gross profit} - \text{Operating expenses}
$$

**Operating margin.**

$$
\text{Operating margin} = \frac{\text{Operating income}}{\text{Net revenue}}
$$

The operating margin shows what percentage of each unit of sales is operating profit.[^sec-guide] See [[Gross Profit]] and [[Operating Expense]].

## Worked Example

**Inputs:** gross profit 7,000, operating expenses 4,790 and revenue 10,000.

**Step 1: operating income.**

$$
7{,}000 - 4{,}790 = 2{,}210
$$

**Step 2: operating margin.**

$$
\frac{2{,}210}{10{,}000} = 22.1\%
$$

The business keeps about 22 of every 100 of sales before lenders and the tax authority take their share.
