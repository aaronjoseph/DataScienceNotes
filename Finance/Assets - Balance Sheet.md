An **asset** is a resource a company controls that is expected to bring future economic benefit. Assets include physical property such as plants, equipment and inventory; intangible items such as trademarks and patents; cash; and investments.[^sec-guide] They are reported on the [[Balance Sheet]].

## Types of Asset

Assets are generally listed by how quickly they turn into cash:[^sec-guide]

- [[Current Asset]] — expected to be converted to cash within one year, such as inventory and receivables.
- [[Non Current Asset]] — not expected to be converted to cash within a year, such as property, plant and equipment.

Both categories can contain tangible and intangible items.
